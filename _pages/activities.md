## Projects

### [PPRBench: A Process-level Benchmark for LLMs' Physical Reasoning](/publication/2026-pprbench)

Developing a contamination-free benchmark for evaluating the process-level physical reasoning of large language models, together with PhysGrader, a rubric-guided framework for step-by-step evaluation.

### [SpectraExpert: Expert-Like Reasoning Agent for Spectrum-Based Molecular Identification](/publication/2026-spectraexpert)

Developing a molecular-identification system that combines domain-adapted Raman spectral retrieval with constrained large language model selection and optional spectroscopic evidence.

### Wearable AI Smart Glasses

Designed and prototyped a low-cost, easily deployable wearable AI smart-glasses system during an internship at the China Telecom Artificial Intelligence Research Institute (Shanghai), Jun–Sep 2024.
